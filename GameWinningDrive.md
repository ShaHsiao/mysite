It fascinates me to learn about American football with strategies and all. From an academic's point of view, I feel like writing a grant proposal is like conducting a football game. Yes, I use the word "conducting", because you have all the tools in your bag, call any play you want, to showcase what you got, it doesn't necessarily mean you can win the game. Everything has to click. The reviewers will tear you apart play by play. All the positions can make mistakes or out of luck, then the play is doomed. The seemingly "game-winning drive" as moments of good decision and execution, surely matters, but it is absolutely not all!!!
